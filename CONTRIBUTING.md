## Features

- Intuitive curve editor for creating custom easing curves with precise control over velocity and timing
- Library of ready-made animation presets for common motion styles such as bounce, elastic, and overshoot
- Real-time preview of applied easing directly on selected keyframes in the timeline
- Save, organize, and reuse your own easing curves across projects and compositions
- Full integration with After Effects keyframes, including copy, paste, and batch application to multiple layers
